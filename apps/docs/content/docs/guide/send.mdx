**Send** has two sides: **A NEAR account** and **Another chain**.

## To a NEAR account

1. Type the amount, or press **All**.
2. Type the NEAR account it goes to (`name.tegata.testnet`, or any NEAR account registered for USDC), or pick one of
   your ready people.
3. Review, then press and hold to send. Your key signs it; Tegata relays it and pays the fee.
4. Live steps follow it to NEAR, then a receipt slip lands with your seal.

The send's receipt is at `/r/send/<id>`: share it with the person you paid. If they have a Tegata account, they get an
email too. Your account never holds NEAR.

## To another chain

In Live, choose a coin and chain from the eleven (USDC on Base to start), paste **Their address on Base**, and the
**Live quote from NEAR Intents** shows what **They get**. The review says **You send exactly**, **They receive**
about, **Arrives**, **Converted by** NEAR Intents and **Network fee: Paid by Tegata**. Press and hold **Hold to send**.

The steps read **Approved with your key**, **Sent from your balance**, **Picked up by NEAR Intents** and
**Delivered on Tron** (or wherever it went), and the receipt, titled "Payment to another chain", links both
transactions. If NEAR Intents can't deliver, the dollars come back to your balance and you get an email. How it works:
[Any chain, in and out](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain#you-send-to-another-chain).

In the Sandbox you see the coins, marked "Live only · NEAR Intents has no testnet", and the button reads **Runs in
Live**. A send is always exact input: you send exactly what you approve and see what arrives.

A [scanned](/docs/guide/scan) NEAR account or wallet address opens Send with it already filled in.
