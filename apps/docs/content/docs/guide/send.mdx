1. Type the amount, or press **All**.
2. Type the NEAR account it goes to (`name.tegata.testnet`, or any NEAR account registered for USDC).
3. Review, then press and hold to send. Your email sign-in's key signs it; Tegata relays it and pays the fee.
4. Live steps follow it to NEAR, then a receipt slip lands with your seal.

The send's receipt is at `/r/send/<id>`: share it with the person you paid. Your account never holds NEAR.

<Callout title="Limits" type="info">
Tegata sponsors up to 30 actions an hour and 200 a day for each person.
</Callout>
