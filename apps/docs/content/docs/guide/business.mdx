Open **Business** in the rail (on a phone, from your account menu or ⌘K). On the left, a live preview of the header
your invoices and receipts carry, with your round business seal; on the right, the fields.

| Field | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | 2 to 60 characters. Your seal follows your name |
| Logo | PNG, JPEG or WebP; shrunk to 256 px in your browser and kept under 200 KB. Remove any time |
| Address | Printed on invoices |
| Tax ID | VAT, EIN, GST…, optional |
| Billing email | Optional; printed on invoices as the address to write to. Your emails go here, or else to your sign-in email |
| Time zone | Picked up when you sign up; due dates, reminders and repeats run in it |

Below them: your pay address (with **Copy**) and your sign-in email, read-only. The button reads **Saved**
until you change something, then **Save changes**.

When you have a logo, the pay page and receipts show it in their header; your seal stays as the PAID stamp.

## Emails

Under the profile, **Emails** shows where your emails go and four switches: **You got paid**, **Payroll is ready to
approve**, **An invoice is overdue** and **Monthly statement**. Money leaving your balance, payroll and security
notices always go. **Recently** lists the last 12 emails with how each went. See [Emails](/docs/guide/emails).
