For taking a payment in person: a till, a market stall, a client at your desk. Open **Counter** from the rail, the
**+** button on a phone, or **⌘K**.

<Phone src="/shots/phone/app-counter.webp" alt="The counter on a phone: Charge $0.00 on the stage, What it's for (optional), the keypad, and Type an amount" caption="The counter, ready for an amount." />

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-counter.webp" alt="The counter on a laptop: Charge $0.00 on the stage, What it's for (optional), the keypad, Type an amount, and Today's sales" caption="The counter on a laptop, for a till." address="tegata.xyz/app/counter" />

<Steps>
<Step>

**Type the amount** on the keypad (two decimals at most), and a note if you want one ("Two flat whites"). The button
reads **Charge $4.50**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Charge.** The stage shows the amount and a QR code of a one-time pay page, with "Scan to pay with USDC on NEAR"
and how long it has been waiting. The customer scans it with their phone's camera and pays on the
[pay page](/docs/guide/pay-page), any way it offers: a Tegata balance, a NEAR wallet, or in Live a coin on another chain.

</Step>
<Step>

**Paid.** When the payment settles on NEAR, the stage turns green, your seal presses PAID and the `received` sound
plays. **New sale** clears the keypad for the next one.

</Step>
</Steps>

**Cancel** leaves a sale unpaid and goes back to the keypad.

## Today's sales

Under the keypad, every counter sale today with its note and time: a paid one shows its amount in green and how it
was paid, and opens the customer's receipt; one not paid yet reads **Waiting** (or **Cancelled**) and opens its pay
page. The counter works the same in
the Sandbox and in Live.

Each sale is a one-time checkout, so it also appears in [Activity](/docs/start/bookkeepers), gets a numbered receipt,
and sends `payment.succeeded` to your [webhooks](/docs/build/developers#webhooks) like any other payment.
