Open it from **Scan** on Home, **Scan a QR code** in the rail, **Scan to pay** under the phone's **+** button, or
**Scan a QR code to pay** in ⌘K. The sheet, **Scan a QR code**, uses your rear camera while it's open. No camera, or
blocked? **Choose a photo** reads a picture or a screenshot of the code instead.

## What it reads

| You scan | It shows | The button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Tegata pay link or counter sale (`/pay/…`) | A pay link | **Open it** |
| A Tegata invoice, receipt, payroll page or join link | An invoice, A receipt, A payroll page, A join link | **Open it** |
| A NEAR account (`near:…`, `name.near`, `name.testnet` or a 64-character implicit account) | A NEAR account | **Send to** the account, in [Send](/docs/guide/send) |
| An Ethereum-style payment code (Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Gnosis, Avalanche), a Bitcoin code, a Solana Pay code, or a bare Tron, `0x`, Bitcoin or Solana address | An address on that chain | **Send** that coin on that chain, in [Send to another chain](/docs/guide/send#to-another-chain) |
| Any other web link | A web link | **Open the link** |
| Anything else | Some text | None: "Tegata can't pay this code." |

A bare `0x` address is read as USDC on Base, and a bare Tron address as USDT on Tron. In the Sandbox, an address on
another chain is marked "Live only · NEAR Intents has no testnet": sending to it runs in Live.

Both screens above are real: the codes were read through **Choose a photo** on the live app, one from Studio
Kaze's own pay link and one from Tegata's testnet account.

Where Tegata draws QR codes for others to scan: **Get paid** (your pay address, `near:<account>`), every
[pay link](/docs/guide/pay-links), every [counter](/docs/guide/counter) sale and your [referral](/docs/guide/refer)
link.
