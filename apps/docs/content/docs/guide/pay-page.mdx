The header shows your logo or seal, the amount, and "receives exactly this in USDC". For an invoice it shows the
number, the due date and **View invoice**; with part-payments allowed the payer types any amount up to what's left.

## Two ways to pay, and a price from other chains

<Tabs items={["Tegata balance", "NEAR wallet", "Another chain"]}>
<Tab value="Tegata balance">

The payer signs in with their email and pays from their own Tegata dollars. Tegata pays the network fee. In the
Sandbox, **Add $5.00 test dollars** appears there when your balance is short. They press and hold **Hold to pay**. You can't pay your own
link from your own balance.

</Tab>
<Tab value="NEAR wallet">

The payer connects a NEAR wallet through NEAR Connect (in the Sandbox, the wallets it lists for testnet, such as
Meteor and Intear; HOT and MyNearWallet are mainnet-only) and approves one USDC transfer to your account carrying this payment's memo. Their wallet pays the network fee.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Another chain">

Coins on Tron, Base, Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, Bitcoin, BNB Chain and Polygon, each with a live NEAR Intents
price for exactly your amount, or the route's reason (such as a minimum). In the Sandbox this stops at the price: NEAR Intents has no testnet, so paying from
another chain is part of Live.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## What happens next

Live steps show the payment moving, then your seal stamps **PAID** and **View your receipt** opens it. The payment
counts only when Tegata reads it from NEAR: see [When is it paid?](/docs/how-money-moves/paid)

A link that is switched off says "This link isn't taking payments". A paid invoice says it is paid and links to
the invoice, where its receipts are.
