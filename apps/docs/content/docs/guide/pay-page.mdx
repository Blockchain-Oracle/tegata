The header shows your logo or seal, the amount, and "receives exactly this in USDC". For an invoice it shows the
number, the due date and **View invoice**; with part-payments allowed the payer types any amount up to what's left.

## Ways to pay

<Tabs items={["Tegata balance", "NEAR wallet", "Another chain", "Card"]}>
<Tab value="Tegata balance">

"Sign in with email · no fees" (marked **Easiest** in the Sandbox). The payer signs in and pays from their own Tegata dollars. Tegata pays
the network fee. In the Sandbox, **Add $5.00 test dollars** appears when the payer's balance is short. They press and
hold **Hold to pay**. You can't pay your own link from your own balance.

</Tab>
<Tab value="NEAR wallet">

The payer connects a NEAR wallet through NEAR Connect ("HOT, Meteor, MyNearWallet, Intear" in Live; "Meteor, Intear
and more" in the Sandbox, where HOT and MyNearWallet don't work) and approves one USDC transfer to your account carrying this payment's memo. Their wallet pays the
network fee.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Another chain">

Pick the coin (USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH or SOL), then the chain: 20 routes on 11 chains, listed on
[Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain#the-routes). Each shows "You would send" with a live NEAR Intents quote for exactly your amount, or the route's
reason (such as its minimum).

**In Live**, the payer gives a refund address on their chain and presses **Get the deposit address**: they send
exactly that amount to it (a QR code carries the address, and on most chains the amount), NEAR Intents converts it,
and you receive exactly what you asked for in USDC on NEAR. Too little, too late, or a route that fails is refunded to
the payer on their own chain. Step by step: [Any chain, in and out](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain).

**In the Sandbox** it stops at the price, marked "Live only · real price": NEAR Intents has no testnet.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Card">

"Visa, Mastercard · about 2 minutes". Transak sells the payer USDC on NEAR, delivered to you; the fee is shown before
the checkout opens ("Card fee, set by Transak"), and "Your card goes to Transak, not to Studio Kaze or Tegata." The
Card row appears only once Tegata has Transak's keys, so payers don't see it today. See
[Cards](/docs/guide/cards#customers-who-pay-by-card).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## What happens next

Live steps show the payment moving, then your seal stamps **PAID** and **View your receipt** opens it. The payment
counts only when Tegata reads it from NEAR: see [When is it paid?](/docs/how-money-moves/paid)
