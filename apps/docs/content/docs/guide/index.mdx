## Home

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-home.webp" alt="Home for Studio Kaze, a demo business on testnet: the grouped rail on the left with Scan a QR code at the top and Bank and Cards under Money; a $1.25 balance with Get paid, Send, Add money and Scan; the pay address card with Copy and Show QR; Latest: Order #1042 paid, a send, a payroll run, invoice INV-0001 paid, Design sprint paid" caption="Home on a laptop, for a demo business signed in with a NEAR wallet." address="tegata.xyz/app" />

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Your USDC on NEAR, read live from the USDC contract. It opens [Balance](/docs/guide/balance) |
| **Get paid** | Your pay address as a QR code, with **Copy** and **Share** |
| **Send** | Opens [Send](/docs/guide/send) |
| **Add money** | Your pay address, a card, another chain, or (in the Sandbox) test dollars: [Balance](/docs/guide/balance#add-money) |
| **Scan** | Opens the camera: [Scan to pay](/docs/guide/scan) |
| Your pay address | **Copy**, and **Show QR** |
| Latest | Payments in, sends, rewards, payroll runs and payslips, newest first. Each row opens its [receipt](/docs/guide/receipts); the receipt links to NEAR |
| Get started | Tiles for a new account: your first invoice, your first pay link, your people, and test dollars while your balance is under $1. Each retires once it's done |
| **Payroll due** | On or after payday, when no run has paid it: what it pays and to how many people |

## Places

On a desktop the rail starts with the [Sandbox / Live](/docs/guide/sandbox) switch, **Search ⌘K** and **Scan a QR
code**, then groups places by job: **Home**, then **Get paid** (Invoices, Pay links, Counter, Customers), **Pay
people** (Send, People, Payroll) and **Money** (Activity, Balance, Bank, Cards), with Refer, Developers and Business at
the foot. A group folds with its chevron, and the rail remembers it.

On a phone the dock holds Home, Invoices, **+**, Pay links and **Menu**. **+** opens New invoice, New pay link, Send,
Run payroll, Counter, Add people, **Scan to pay** and Get paid (QR); **Menu** opens every place, with search on top.

| Place | For |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Balance and Latest |
| Invoices | [Invoices](/docs/guide/invoices) |
| Pay links | [Pay links](/docs/guide/pay-links) |
| Counter | [Take a payment in person](/docs/guide/counter): keypad, QR code, paid |
| Customers | [Customers](/docs/guide/customers) |
| Send | [Send](/docs/guide/send), to a NEAR account or another chain |
| People | [Your team and where their pay lands](/docs/guide/pay-people) |
| Payroll | [The schedule, runs and payslips](/docs/guide/pay-people#run-payroll) |
| Activity | Every movement by month, filters, search, refunds, the CSV and the statement ([for bookkeepers](/docs/start/bookkeepers)) |
| Balance | [What you hold, Add money, Move out, and the books checked against NEAR](/docs/guide/balance) |
| Bank | [Withdraw to a bank, and a euro account](/docs/guide/bank) |
| Cards | [Cards for you and your team, and a crypto card you already have](/docs/guide/cards) |
| Refer | [Referrals](/docs/guide/refer) |
| Developers | [API keys, checkout sessions and webhooks](/docs/build/developers) |
| Business | [Your business profile](/docs/guide/business) and its [emails](/docs/guide/emails) |

**⌘K** (or Search on a phone) opens every action and place from the keyboard, including **Scan a QR code to pay**
and **Withdraw to a bank · bank details**.

## Sandbox and Live

The switch at the top says which you're in: the **Sandbox**, test dollars on NEAR testnet, or **Live**, real
dollars on NEAR mainnet, paid in from any chain. See [Sandbox and Live](/docs/guide/sandbox).

A page that isn't there gets a returned bill: "Bill No. 404 · A page that isn't here", stamped **Returned**, with
**Open Tegata** and **Home**.
