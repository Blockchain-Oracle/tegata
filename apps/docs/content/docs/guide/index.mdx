## Home

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Your USDC on NEAR, read live from the USDC contract |
| **Get paid** | Your pay address as a QR code, with **Copy** and **Share** |
| **Send** | Opens [Send](/docs/guide/send) |
| **Copy** | Copies your pay address |
| **On NEAR** | Opens your account on the NEAR explorer |
| Latest | Payments in, payments out, sends, rewards, payroll runs and payslips, newest first. Each row opens its [receipt](/docs/guide/receipts); the receipt links to NEAR |
| Get started | Tiles for a new account: your first invoice, your first pay link, your people, and test dollars while your balance is under $1. Each retires once it's done |
| **Payroll due** | On or after payday, when no run has paid it: what it pays and to how many people |

## Places

On a desktop the rail on the left holds every place; on a phone the dock holds Home, Invoices, **+** (New invoice,
New pay link, Send, Run payroll, Add people, Get paid), Pay links and Search.

| Place | For |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Balance and Latest |
| Get paid | Your pay address and QR code |
| Invoices | [Invoices](/docs/guide/invoices) |
| Pay links | [Pay links](/docs/guide/pay-links) |
| Customers | [Customers](/docs/guide/customers) |
| Activity | Every movement by month, filters, search, the CSV and the statement ([for bookkeepers](/docs/start/bookkeepers)) |
| Balance | What you hold and the books checked against NEAR ([for bookkeepers](/docs/start/bookkeepers)) |
| People | [Your team and where their pay lands](/docs/guide/pay-people) |
| Payroll | [The schedule, runs and payslips](/docs/guide/pay-people#run-payroll) |
| Send | [Send](/docs/guide/send) |
| Refer | [Referrals](/docs/guide/refer) |
| Business | [Your business profile](/docs/guide/business) |

**⌘K** (or Search on a phone) opens every action and place from the keyboard.

## Sandbox and Live

The switch at the top of the rail (and of the phone bar) says you're in the **Sandbox**: test dollars on NEAR
testnet. **Live** carries a lock: it isn't built yet. See
[Sandbox and Live](/docs/guide/sandbox).
