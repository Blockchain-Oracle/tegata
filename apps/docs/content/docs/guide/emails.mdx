Each email is queued in an outbox with its own key, so the same event never sends two. The worker sends the outbox
every 3 seconds through Resend, tries each email up to 6 times with growing waits, and drops anything more than 24
hours old.

Emails to you come from Tegata. Emails to your customers and your people come from **"Studio Kaze via Tegata"**
(your business's name), and replies reach you: your billing email, or else the email you sign in with.

## Business → Emails

Open **Business** and scroll to **Emails**. It shows where your emails go ("Sent to …": your billing email, or else the
email you sign in with), the switches, and **Recently**: the last 12 emails with **Sent**, **Failed**, **Off** or **Sending**.

| Switch | What it sends |
| --- | --- |
| **You got paid** | Each payment you receive |
| **Payroll is ready to approve** | Once per payday, from 08:00 in your time zone, while no run has paid it: what the run will pay. Never for a payday autopilot pays |
| **An invoice is overdue** | When a reminder goes out after the due date |
| **Monthly statement** | On the 1st (08:00 UTC): last month in and out, with the statement PDF |

**Money leaving your balance**, **Payroll** and **Security notices** are marked **Always**: they can't be switched
off.

## Every email

| Who gets it | The emails |
| --- | --- |
| Your customer | Your receipt · an invoice (with its PDF) · a reminder · an invoice cancelled · an invoice marked paid by hand · a refund |
| Your people | Added to payroll (and a reminder to choose where pay lands) · You've been paid (with the payslip PDF, or a contractor's payment receipt) · Your pay will go to… from (a change waiting 24 hours) · Your pay was set by the business |
| You, money in | You got paid · arrived by bank transfer · a payment from another chain that was refunded to the payer · a referral reward |
| You, money out | Sent · Delivered to another chain · didn't arrive, back in your balance · refund sent · paid to a bank · a withdrawal that didn't go through · payroll paid, or a batch that didn't go through |
| You, the business | Welcome · Live is on · payroll is ready · an invoice is overdue · autopilot is on, or short · someone joined with your link, or qualified · a new API key · a webhook that keeps failing · the monthly statement |

Payments land as a **slip** on Tegata's vermilion stage (the amount, who paid whom, the rows of the receipt, and a
link to it); everything else is a short **card**. A NEAR send to another Tegata account also emails that account's
owner.
