The switch at the top of the rail (and of the phone bar) says which you're in. **Sandbox** is said in words and a
dashed rim, never a colour. Tap **Live** and the app reloads in Live; tap **Sandbox** to come back. Your choice is
kept for a year on that browser.

## The two side by side

| | Sandbox | Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network | NEAR testnet | NEAR mainnet |
| Your account | `name.tegata.testnet` | `name.tegata.near` |
| Money | Circle's test USDC | Circle's USDC |
| Tegata's operator, paying every fee | `tegata.testnet` | `tegata.near` |
| Paying in from another chain | The pay page shows NEAR Intents' real price, then "Live only" | Works, through [NEAR Intents](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain) |
| Sending to another chain, refunds to the payer's chain, payroll on another chain, adding money from another chain | Locked, marked "Live only" | Works, through NEAR Intents |
| [Bank](/docs/guide/bank) | On Noah's sandbox | Opens after Noah's business review |
| [Autopilot](/docs/how-money-moves/autopilot) | Tegata's contract `pay.tegata.testnet`; testnet payday proved | Tegata's contract `pay.tegata.near` deployed; no Live payout recorded yet |
| [Pay by card](/docs/guide/cards#customers-who-pay-by-card) | Transak's test checkout; Tegata sends the test USDC | Transak delivers the USDC |
| Add test dollars | $5.00 of test USDC, once a day | None |
| [Referral](/docs/guide/refer) rewards | $1 each after $5 | $10 each after $50 |
| [API keys](/docs/build/developers) | `tk_test_…` | `tk_live_…` |
| Transactions open on | testnet.nearblocks.io | nearblocks.io |

Cards and pay by card both wait on their partners' keys in either mode: see [Cards](/docs/guide/cards).

## Opening your Live account

The first time you switch to Live, Tegata says **Open your Live account.** ("Real dollars from any chain, with no
fees to pay.") and shows the account it will open. Press **Open name.tegata.near**: the steps create it on NEAR
mainnet with your own key, register it for USDC and cut your seal, as on your first day. Tegata pays for all of it.
**Back to the Sandbox** is always there. When it's ready you get an email: "Live is on: Studio Kaze can take real
dollars".

It's a second NEAR account under the same sign-in. Each mode keeps its own invoices, pay links, people, payroll,
books, cards and API keys; your business profile and your customers are shared.

<Callout title="Signed in with a NEAR wallet?" type="warn">
Wallet sign-in uses NEAR testnet today, so a wallet account can use the Sandbox but can't open Live: Live asks for a
mainnet NEAR wallet ("Your wallet account is on testnet. Sign in with a mainnet NEAR wallet to use Live."), and the
sign-in page doesn't offer one yet. Sign in with email to use Live.
</Callout>

## Sandbox

- Nothing costs real money.
- **Add test dollars** sends you $5.00 of test USDC from Tegata's stash, once a day. It's on Home while your balance
  is under $1, under **Add money**, and on the pay page whenever your balance is short.
- If the stash runs dry, [Circle's faucet](https://faucet.circle.com) gives 20 test USDC to any NEAR testnet address
  (choose NEAR Testnet).
- Paying from another chain shows its live NEAR Intents price and stops there: NEAR Intents has no testnet.
