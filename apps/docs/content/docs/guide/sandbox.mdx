The switch at the top of the app shows which you're in. **Sandbox** is said in words and a dashed rim, never a
colour. **Live** carries a lock: it isn't built yet, and tapping it says so.

## Sandbox

- Accounts are `name.tegata.testnet`; money is Circle's test USDC on NEAR testnet.
- Nothing costs real money.
- **Add test dollars** sends you $5.00 of test USDC from Tegata's stash, once a day. It's on Home while your
  balance is under $1, and on the pay page whenever your balance is short.
- If the stash runs dry, [Circle's faucet](https://faucet.circle.com) gives 20 test USDC to any NEAR testnet
  address (choose NEAR Testnet).
- Paying from another chain shows its live NEAR Intents price and stops there: NEAR Intents has no testnet.

## Live

Live (real dollars on NEAR mainnet, accounts as `name.tegata.near`, and paying in from other chains through NEAR
Intents) is planned and not built yet. It needs Tegata's mainnet operator account, `tegata.near`, and the mainnet
side of the app.
