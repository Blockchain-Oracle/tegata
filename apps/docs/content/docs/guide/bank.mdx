Open **Bank** under **Money** (or **Withdraw to a bank · bank details** in ⌘K, or **To a bank** on
[Balance](/docs/guide/balance)). Two tabs: **Withdraw** and **Receive**. Only the owner can use them.

Bank runs in the Sandbox on Noah's test bank, marked **Sandbox · Noah's test bank**. The rates, fees, bank lists and
account names on this page are Noah's own Sandbox answers, live. In Live the page says "Live bank payouts open after
Noah's business review. Try it in the Sandbox."

## Withdraw to a bank

| Country | Money | Banks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | Naira, ₦ (NGN) | Noah's full list (571 in the Sandbox), with **Test account** for Noah's test bank account |
| Ghana | Cedi, GH₵ (GHS) | Noah's list |
| South Africa | Rand, R (ZAR) | Noah's list |

<Steps>
<Step>
### Type the dollars

**You send** in dollars (or **Use all**). Under it, Noah's rate ("1 USD = ₦1,351.70"), **Noah's fee** in local money
and "Arrives in about a minute"; **They get** shows what lands in the bank. Below Noah's minimum it says how much
at least ("At least $1.10 to this bank"). The flag pill changes the country.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add who you're paying

**New recipient** opens **Recipient in Nigeria**: **Choose their bank** (popular banks first, every one with its
logo, or its initials when it has none), then the account number. In Nigeria the bank names the holder: after ten
digits Tegata asks Noah, and the name comes back in a green chip, "from the bank". A wrong number says "The bank
didn't find this account. Check the number."

In Ghana and South Africa you say who holds it (**A person** or **A business**) and their name; South Africa also asks
their email, phone and address. Then **What it's for** (Salary, Supplier, Services, Family, Other), an optional
reference for their statement, and **Use this account**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and hold

**Continue** checks the account with Noah. If the bank names someone else, or nobody was named yet, Tegata asks "The
bank says it's …" with **Not them** and **Pay them** first. Then the review shows what they get, the rate, Noah's
fee, "Network fee · Paid by Tegata" and when it arrives. Press and hold **Hold to withdraw**.
</Step>
<Step>
### On its way

**Approved** (with your key, on this device), **Left your balance** (on NEAR, fee paid by Tegata), **On its way to
Noah** (as USDC on Solana) and **Paid to** the holder. Then a **Paid to a bank** slip, stamped with your seal, with
links **On NEAR** and **To Noah**. If it doesn't go through, the dollars come back to your balance.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Receive: a euro account

<Shot src="/shots/wide/ex-bank-receive.webp" alt="Example: Receive on a laptop: Euro account, IBAN · SEPA transfers, Active; account holder Studio Kaze, an IBAN, a BIC and the bank, with Copy all and Share; Euro transfers arrive as dollars in your balance; US dollar account, ACH and wire, Live; Send a test transfer with €2, €5 and €10; Arrived: €2.00, +$1.16, in your balance" caption="Example: the euro account and a test transfer that arrived as dollars." address="tegata.xyz/app/bank" />

**Get euro bank details** opens a euro account at Noah in your business's name: an IBAN for SEPA transfers, with the
account holder, BIC and bank, each tap-to-copy, **Copy all** and **Share**. "Euro transfers arrive as dollars in your
balance": Noah turns each one into USDC, and Tegata credits the same dollars to your USDC on NEAR within about a
minute. Each deposit is listed under **Arrived**, as **In your balance** once it's credited.

In the Sandbox, **Send a test transfer** sends €2, €5 or €10 through Noah's own deposit flow. A US dollar account (ACH
and wire) is shown locked for Live.

## In your books

Withdrawals and deposits are rows in **Activity** with the country's flag: "To Access Bank ••0031 · ₦…" and "Bank
transfer · €…", each opening **Bank**. The in-page slip is the receipt. Emails tell you when a withdrawal is paid,
when one didn't go through, and when a bank transfer arrived ([Emails](/docs/guide/emails)).
