## Build one

Open **Invoices** and press **New invoice**, or start from the landing page's **Try an invoice, no sign-up**: the
draft stays on your device and opens in your account when you sign in to send it.

| Field | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| Bill to | Pick a customer, or add one inline (name, email, address, tax ID) |
| Items | Description, quantity (up to three decimals, for hours), price; up to 50 items |
| Discount | A percent or a dollar amount, never more than the subtotal |
| Tax rate | One rate, on what's left after the discount |
| Dates | Issue date, and a due date from the terms chips or a date |
| Notes | Up to 1,000 characters |
| **Allow part-payments** | The customer may pay any amount up to what's left |
| **Send reminders** | "3 days before, on the day, then 3 and 7 days late, at 9:00" in your time zone, by email to the customer (needs a customer email). Each step has its own words, below |
| **Repeat** | Weekly or monthly: the next invoice issues itself (a number and a link) shortly after 09:00 in your time zone; with email on, it is also sent to the customer |

The preview has three tabs: **Invoice** (the paper), **Pay page** (the real page your customer opens) and
**Email** (the real email).

## Money rules

- A line is quantity × price, rounded to the cent. Amounts are kept as exact whole cents of USDC.
- Total = subtotal − discount + tax.
- The number (INV-0001…) is given when you send, from a counter that can't hand out the same number twice.

## Statuses

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Not sent yet; no number |
| Sent | Sent, nobody has opened it |
| Viewed | Opened by someone other than you |
| Partly paid | Some of it received (on NEAR or marked paid by hand), not yet due |
| Overdue | Past its due date in your time zone, not paid |
| Paid | Paid in full; stamped with your seal |
| Void | Cancelled; its link stops taking payments |

Viewed, Partly paid and Overdue are worked out each time the invoice is read, so they never go stale. Each step
(created, sent, viewed, each payment, paid, voided, emailed) writes a line on the invoice's **Timeline**.

## After sending

- **Copy link**, **Share** and **Open**: the customer's link opens the invoice with a **Pay** button for what's left
  (from a Tegata balance, a NEAR wallet, or in Live any coin on another chain), and **Download PDF**.
- **Paid by hand**: opens **Mark paid by hand**, which records money **Received outside Tegata**, with an amount and
  a note. Its receipt says it isn't on NEAR.
- **Duplicate**: a fresh draft with the same customer and items.
- **Void**: cancels it; the number stays used.

## Emails and reminders

Sending an invoice emails it to the customer with its PDF attached ("INV-0007 from Studio Kaze: $1,250.00 due 30
November 2026"), when they have an email; either way you get its link to share. With **Send reminders** on, each step
has its own subject:

| When | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| 3 days before | Reminder: INV-0007 from Studio Kaze, $1,250.00 due 30 November 2026 |
| On the day | INV-0007 from Studio Kaze is due today: $1,250.00 |
| 3 days late | INV-0007 from Studio Kaze is 3 days overdue: $1,250.00 |
| 7 days late | Final reminder: INV-0007 from Studio Kaze, $1,250.00 |

Each reminder asks for what's still left, and the ones after the due date add "Already paid? Ignore this. Reminders
stop once the payment shows on NEAR." Right before one goes out, Tegata checks again: a paid or voided invoice, or one
with reminders switched off since, gets none.

On a sent invoice, the **Reminders** row shows the next one ("Next: Mon 30 Nov, 09:00 · on the due date"), "Off" or
"All sent", with a switch for the scheduled ones and **Remind now**, which sends today's reminder at once, worded by
how late the invoice is, at most once a day. A customer without an email gets none, and the row says why.

The invoice's timeline names each one ("Reminder sent, 3 days before it was due", "Reminder sent on the due date",
"Reminder sent, 3 days late", "Final reminder sent", "Reminder sent by you"). Once a reminder goes out after the due
date, you get "An invoice is overdue" too, unless you've switched it off in [Emails](/docs/guide/emails). Replies to
any of these reach you: your billing email, or else the email you sign in with.
