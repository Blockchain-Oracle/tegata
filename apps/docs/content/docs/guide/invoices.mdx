## Build one

Open **Invoices** and press **New invoice**, or start from the landing page's **Try an invoice, no sign-up**: the
draft stays on your device and opens in your account when you sign in to send it.

| Field | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| Bill to | Pick a customer, or add one inline (name, email, address, tax ID) |
| Items | Description, quantity (up to three decimals, for hours), price; up to 50 items |
| Discount | A percent or a dollar amount, never more than the subtotal |
| Tax rate | One rate, on what's left after the discount |
| Dates | Issue date, and a due date from the terms chips or a date |
| Notes | Up to 1,000 characters |
| **Allow part-payments** | The customer may pay any amount up to what's left |
| **Send reminders** | Emails the customer 3 days before, on, and 3 and 7 days after the due date, at 09:00 in your time zone. Needs email switched on and a customer email, so it's off on the live Sandbox |
| **Repeat** | Weekly or monthly: the next invoice issues itself (a number and a link) shortly after 09:00 in your time zone; with email on, it is also sent to the customer |

The preview has three tabs: **Invoice** (the paper), **Pay page** (the real page your customer opens) and
**Email** (the real email).

## Money rules

- A line is quantity × price, rounded to the cent. Amounts are kept as exact whole cents of USDC.
- Total = subtotal − discount + tax.
- The number (INV-0001…) is given when you send, from a counter that can't hand out the same number twice.

## Statuses

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Not sent yet; no number |
| Sent | Sent, nobody has opened it |
| Viewed | Opened by someone other than you |
| Partly paid | Some of it received (on NEAR or marked paid by hand), not yet due |
| Overdue | Past its due date in your time zone, not paid |
| Paid | Paid in full; stamped with your seal |
| Void | Cancelled; its link stops taking payments |

Viewed, Partly paid and Overdue are worked out each time the invoice is read, so they never go stale. Each step
(created, sent, viewed, each payment, paid, voided, emailed) writes a line on the invoice's **Timeline**.

## After sending

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-invoice.webp" alt="Invoice INV-0001, partly paid: the invoice from Studio Kaze to Northwind Ltd for $0.30 on the stage; beside it $0.15 received and $0.15 left to pay, Billed to Northwind Ltd, the actions, one payment from a NEAR wallet, and the timeline: drafted, sent, $0.15 received on NEAR" caption="A real invoice paid in part: what's left, who it's billed to, the payment and the timeline." address="tegata.xyz/app/invoices/…" />

- **Copy link** and **Share**: the customer's link opens the invoice with a **Pay** button for what's left, and
  **Download PDF**.
- **Mark paid by hand**: records money received another way, with an amount and a note. Its receipt says it isn't
  on NEAR.
- **Duplicate**: a fresh draft with the same customer and items.
- **Void**: cancels it; the number stays used.

<Callout title="Email is off on the live site" type="info">
Invoice and reminder emails are built but switched off on the live Sandbox until a sending domain is set up, so
reminders don't go out either. Sending an invoice still gives you its link to share.
</Callout>
