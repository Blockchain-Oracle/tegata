## Add your team

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-people.webp" alt="People for Studio Kaze: pay per run $0.20, 2 people, 2 ready, 0 waiting; Kofi Mensah, Developer, Ready, $0.10 and Mei Tanaka, Designer, Ready, $0.10" caption="People: who you pay, and whether each is ready." address="tegata.xyz/app/people" />

Open **People** and press **Add person**: name, email, role (optional) and pay per run in dollars. Or press
**Import CSV** with a header row naming `name`, `email`, `role` and `amount`:

```csv
name,email,role,amount
Mei Tanaka,mei@studio.example,Designer,2400
Kofi Mensah,kofi@studio.example,Engineer,"3,100.50"
```

Every row is shown before anything is saved. A row with a mistake says which line and what to fix ("Line 3:
"leo@example" isn't an email"), and only the good rows are imported. A team holds up to 200 people.

Each person gets their own page at `/p/<token>`. Copy or share it from their sheet. A person with an email also gets
it by email ("Studio Kaze added you to payroll"), and a reminder if a run skips them because they haven't chosen.

## Where their pay lands

The person opens their page and chooses:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Tegata account** (Recommended) | "Your own dollar account. No wallet, no fees." They sign in with the email they were invited with, name themselves, and their own Tegata account opens (`name.tegata.testnet`, or `name.tegata.near` in Live) |
| **NEAR account** | "One you already have" (`name.testnet` or `name.near`). Tegata checks it exists and, if it can't hold USDC yet, registers it and pays for that |
| **Another chain** | "USDT, USDC, BTC… through NEAR Intents". In Live: a coin and chain from the eleven, their address there, and **Get paid in USDT on Tron** (or whichever). In the Sandbox it's locked with a **Live** badge, because NEAR Intents has no testnet |
| **Bank account** | "Cash out to your own bank", shown with the flags of Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa and marked **Coming**: a person can't choose a bank yet. The business can already [withdraw to a bank](/docs/guide/bank) itself |

You can also set it for them from their sheet: a NEAR account, or in Live **Pay them on another chain**.

Only someone signed in with the invited email can choose, and the first person to choose is bound to that invite.
**A change waits 24 hours** once they already have a destination. The business sees "Changes Fri 17:21" on their
row, and pay keeps landing in the old place until then. Their first choice, and anything you set for them from their
sheet, apply at once.

## Run payroll

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set a payday

Open **Payroll**: weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and the next payday, in your business's time zone. A monthly
payday on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review

**Run payroll** drafts the run from your team as it is now: each person, the account their pay lands in, and this
run's amount. Switch anyone off to leave them out. People who aren't ready are skipped with the reason ("Hasn't
chosen where pay lands", "Their Tegata account is still opening"). The total is shown against your balance.
</Step>
<Step>
### Hold to pay

Your key signs one gas-free action that carries one USDC transfer per person, each with the memo
`tegata:payroll:<row>`. Before signing, the page re-encodes it and checks every transfer against the rows on
screen. Tegata relays it and pays the network fee.

In Live, a person paid on another chain gets a fresh, signed NEAR Intents quote when you approve; their transfer in
the same action goes to that quote's deposit address instead. Paid in USDT or USDC, they receive exactly their pay:
"Rows on another chain arrive in full. NEAR Intents' conversion, at most 2% or $1 each, is added to what you send."
See [Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain#your-team-paid-on-another-chain).
</Step>
<Step>
### Everyone, or nobody

On NEAR, every transfer in one action list lands together or none do. Rows turn **Paid** only when the USDC
contract's own event shows the transfer to that account, for that amount, with that memo. Then each gets a payslip
at `/r/slip/<row>`. More than 50 people go in batches of 50, one hold signs them all, and each batch is all or
nothing. A row on another chain reads **On its way** until NEAR Intents delivers it, and only then is **Paid**, with
its payslip; if it comes back, it reads **Not paid**, the USDC is back in your balance, and paying the run again sends
it.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a batch fails, nothing in it moved. The run says so, and **Try again** drafts it fresh. People already paid stay
paid and are never paid twice.

## Payslips

Every paid row gets a payslip at `/r/slip/<row>`, stamped with the business's seal, with **Download PDF** and **Save as
PDF**. The person's own page and your sheet for them list every payslip with a download button, and the "You've been
paid" email carries the PDF.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | The business, the person and their role, the pay period, pay date, when it was paid and how often |
| Gross, deductions, net | Gross pay, deductions $0.00, net pay, and the PAID seal |
| Earnings | This period, and the year to date |
| Deductions | Income tax and pension and social contributions, each "Not withheld"; Tegata and network fees "Paid by Tegata". Tegata doesn't calculate or withhold tax, and the payslip says so |
| How it was paid | USDC on NEAR, the account and the NEAR transaction; for another chain, what NEAR Intents delivered and the delivery transaction |

## Or let autopilot pay

Turn on [Autopilot](/docs/how-money-moves/autopilot) on the Payroll page and the paydays you choose are paid by
Tegata's own contract on NEAR at 09:00 in your time zone, with nobody online. Those paydays show **Autopilot pays this
one** instead of **Run payroll**, so a payday is never paid twice. Autopilot runs in the Sandbox; in Live the card says
"Live opens when its contract is on NEAR mainnet · runs in the Sandbox now". It never pays people on another chain:
they're paid when you approve a run.

## A real one

A proof business added Mei and Kofi by hand and Sara from a CSV (the same CSV's second row, with a bad email, was
refused). Mei opened her own Tegata account; Kofi gave a NEAR account that couldn't hold USDC yet, so Tegata
registered it (<Tx hash="G9ZMLtY9grbqzwva6hhUjtFxqUErcAeEw4p4qjFPpad7" />). One approval paid Mei $0.50 and Kofi
$0.30 in a single transaction (<Tx hash="7hEg9DgZdEaq7e4AHtY9FZ4ReB3sveq5XwewiEanCKFr" />), and Sara, who hadn't
chosen, was skipped. Run it yourself with `pnpm --filter @tegata/services exec tsx src/proofs/payroll.ts`.

<Callout title="Where it shows up" type="info">
Home shows **Payroll due** on or after payday when no run has paid it, and an email says "Payroll is ready" from 08:00
your time. Activity lists each run for the business and each payslip for the person. **Send** offers your ready
people as one-tap recipients. People get their invite and their payslips by [email](/docs/guide/emails).
</Callout>
