Open **Customers**, or add one inline from the invoice builder's **Bill to**.

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Required |
| Email (optional) | Where their invoices, reminders and receipts go |
| Address | Printed on the invoice |
| Tax ID (optional) | Printed on the invoice |

The stage shows what your customers owe you, how many of them owe, and what they've paid you. Search by name or
email. Each row, on its own coloured disc, shows the customer's email, how many invoices you've sent them and what they
owe. Their sheet shows **Billed**, **Paid** and **Owed**, lets you edit them, and starts a new invoice for them.
