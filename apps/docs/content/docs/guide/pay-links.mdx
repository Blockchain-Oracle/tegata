## Make one

Open **Pay links** and press **New pay link**.

| Field | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| What it's for | The title the payer sees |
| Amount | A fixed amount, or turn on **Any amount** and the payer types it |
| Note for the payer | Optional |

## A link's page

- The link card with your seal, and its QR code (the 手形 seal in the middle).
- **Copy**, **Share** and **Open**.
- **Taking payments**: switch it off and the link stops taking payments; switch it on again any time.
- **Payments**: each payment as it arrives, opening its receipt. The total received sits above.

A link can be paid many times. Each payment gets its own receipt.

To sell from your own site, put the link behind a pay button: **Checkout** under **Get paid** builds the code, and
[Checkout](/docs/build/checkout) explains it.
