Every row on Home opens its receipt first; the receipt then links to the transaction on NEAR. Receipts are public
pages: share one with the person you paid, or the one who paid you.

| Kind | Address | Numbered | Stamp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer payment | `/r/<token>` | Yes, No. 0001… per business | Your seal, PAID |
| Send | `/r/send/<id>` | No | Your seal, SENT |
| Referral reward | `/r/reward/<id>` | No | Tegata's seal |

Each one shows **Paid to**, **For**, **From**, **Paid with**, **Settled** (in the reader's own time zone, named),
**Network fee** and **Transaction**,
then **Share**, **Save as PDF** and **See it on NEAR**. A send that hasn't settled shows "Settling" and no stamp;
a failed send moved no money and has no receipt.
