Every row on Home opens its receipt first; the receipt then links to the transaction on NEAR. Receipts are public
pages: share one with the person you paid, or the one who paid you.

| Kind | Address | Numbered | Stamp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer payment | `/r/<token>` | Yes, No. 0001… per business | Your seal, PAID (or Refunded) |
| Send, payment to another chain, refund | `/r/send/<id>` | No | Your seal, SENT |
| Referral reward | `/r/reward/<id>` | No | Tegata's seal |
| Payslip | `/r/slip/<id>`, and its PDF at `/r/slip/<id>/pdf` | No | The business's seal, PAID |

Each one shows **Paid to**, **For**, **From**, **Paid with**, **Settled** (in the reader's own time zone, named),
**Network fee** and **Transaction**, then **Share**, **Save as PDF** and **See it on NEAR**. A send that hasn't
settled shows "Status · Waiting for NEAR" and no stamp; a failed send moved no money and has no receipt. A payslip
also has **Download PDF**: gross to net, the year to date and how it was paid ([Payslips](/docs/guide/pay-people#payslips)).

## How it was paid

| Paid with | What the receipt adds |
| --- | --- |
| Tegata balance | Network fee: Paid by Tegata |
| NEAR wallet | Network fee: Paid by the payer |
| Another chain (Live) | "USDT on Tron → USDC on NEAR", **Converted by** NEAR Intents, network fee paid by the payer; **From** is their refund address |
| Card | "Card · through Transak" and **Card through**: Transak (in the Sandbox: "Transak test checkout · test USDC sent by Tegata") |
| Marked paid by hand | "Not on NEAR", and no transaction |

A payment to another chain is titled "Payment to another chain" (or "Refund"), with **Delivered as** and a link to the
delivery transaction on that chain. A payslip paid on another chain adds **Delivered as** too.

Bank withdrawals and deposits have no public receipt page: their slip on [Bank](/docs/guide/bank) and the email are
the record.
