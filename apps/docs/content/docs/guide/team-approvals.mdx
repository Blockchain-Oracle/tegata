A rule only Tegata checks would be a promise: the owner's key could still move the money. With team approvals the
payroll money sits in a **team treasury** on NEAR, a Sputnik DAO v2 contract that pays only when *k* of *n* approvers
vote yes. Each approver votes with their own key, through Tegata's relayer, so nobody pays a network fee. Tegata isn't
in the treasury's rules at all: it can't propose a payment, vote, or move what the treasury holds.

## Set it up

1. **Business → Team → Add an approver.** Their name and email. They get an email with a link to `/team/<token>`,
   sign in with that email (their own Tegata account opens if they don't have one) and press **Join as an approver**.
   An invite stays open for two weeks. After that its link says it has expired, and Team shows **Expired** with
   **Send again**, which emails them a fresh link (the old one never works again).
2. **Pick the rule.** "Payroll needs **2** of **3** approvals". You count as one of them. At least 2.
3. **Open the team treasury** once everyone has joined. Tegata creates it on NEAR and registers it for USDC
   (Tegata pays both), then you **hold to seal it**: one action that switches off code updates and locks the rule,
   so changing anything later needs the same *k* of *n* approvals.
4. **Add money**: one gas-free transfer from your balance into the treasury. From then on it only leaves by approvals.

On Live the treasury is `<name>.sputnik-dao.near`, from the same factory Trezu uses, so it also opens in
[Trezu](https://trezu.app) for anyone with a wallet for their account. In the Sandbox it is `<name>-team.tegata.testnet`,
running the same contract code.

## Paying payroll with approvals

- Review the run as usual. The hold reads **Hold to send for approval**: it files the payroll on the treasury (one
  USDC transfer per person, each with the memo `tegata:payroll:<row>`) together with your own yes.
- Every other approver gets an email, and the run waits in their **Approvals** (in the rail under Pay people, and ⌘K).
  They see who it pays and who has voted, then **hold to approve** or **decline**.
- The vote that reaches *k* makes the treasury pay everyone in one batch: every row lands or none does. Rows turn paid
  only from USDC's own transfer events in that transaction, the same rule as every payment in Tegata, and payslips go
  out as usual.
- Votes cast in Trezu count the same. If the deciding one is cast there, Tegata finds the payout on NEAR within a
  minute or so and marks the run paid, from the same USDC events.
- Enough declines end it as **Declined**; a proposal left open for 7 days **Expires**. Nothing moves either way, and
  the owner can send it again.
- If the approved batch itself fails on NEAR (the treasury was short, say), nothing moves and the run shows
  **Failed**. Add money to the treasury and **hold to try again**: the same proposal is finalized again, so the same
  approvals pay everyone, all or nobody. That works until its 7 days end; after that it can be sent again as a fresh
  proposal. Tegata never files a second proposal while the first can still pay, so nobody is paid twice.

Before anyone signs, their browser re-reads the action: the payroll's transfers must be exactly the rows on screen, on
the USDC contract, on this business's treasury. A vote also names the exact proposal it approves, so it can't land on
another one.

## Limits, said plainly

- The treasury pays NEAR accounts (Tegata accounts and your people's own NEAR accounts). A row for another chain is
  paid from your own balance, outside the treasury.
- Up to 50 people per proposal: the vote that pays carries the gas for every transfer.
- Approvers are fixed when the treasury opens. Changing them is a proposal the current approvers approve; the screen
  for that comes later.
- The treasury keeps a little NEAR for the proposals it stores; Tegata tops it up.
