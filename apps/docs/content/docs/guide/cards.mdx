Three things carry a card in Tegata: **spend cards** your team pays with, **a crypto card you already have**, and
**customers paying you by card**. All three settle in USDC on NEAR.

<Callout title="Opening soon" type="warn">
Cards are issued by Bitnob, and issuing waits on Bitnob's sandbox keys. Until they're set, **Cards** shows "Cards for
you and your team" with **Opening soon** and no **New card** button. The screens marked Example show the finished
design on sample data.
</Callout>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Who gets one | You (the card carries your business's name), or anyone in [People](/docs/guide/pay-people) who has an email |
| The card | A virtual Visa by Bitnob. Each card spends only what you put on it |
| Filling it | From your balance, at least $2.00 a time, in one gas-free transfer |
| **Add money** / **Move back** | Put more on the card, or bring what's left back to your balance |
| **Freeze** | Stops the card at once, until you unfreeze it |
| Monthly limit | A cap on what the card spends each month, or **No limit** |
| Purchases | Each purchase arrives from Bitnob as it happens: **Paid**, **Pending**, **Reversed**, or **Declined** with the reason ("card frozen") |
| **Test buy** | Sandbox only: a test purchase on the card, so you can see one arrive |

Only the owner makes and manages cards.

<Phone src="/shots/phone/ex-card-new.webp" alt="Example: the New card sheet: a black Studio Kaze Visa, For: You, Mei Tanaka, Kofi Mensah, Sara Lind; Card name; Start with $20; Monthly limit, No limit; Hold to create and add $20.00" caption="Example: a new card, for you or someone on your team." />

**New card** asks who it's for, a name ("Ads, Travel, Supplies…"), how much to start with and a monthly limit. Press
and hold **Hold to create and add $20.00**: your key signs one transfer to Tegata's card reserve, then the steps
read **Approved**, **$20.00 to the card** and **Making the card · Visa, by Bitnob**, and **Your card is ready**.

How the money moves, and who holds it at each step:
[Bank and cards](/docs/how-money-moves/bank-and-cards).

## A crypto card you already have

<Phone src="/shots/phone/cards-gnosis.webp" alt="The Your crypto card sheet on the live app: Link your Gnosis Pay card, a Gnosis Pay card spends the USDC in its own account on the Gnosis chain; Name (optional); Card account address on Gnosis, 0x…; Link the card" caption="Link a Gnosis Pay card by its account address." />

A Gnosis Pay card spends the dollars in its own account on the Gnosis chain. Press **Already have a crypto card? Top
it up**, paste the card account's address (it starts `0x`) and press **Link the card**. Tegata reads its balance
from Gnosis (USDC.e and EURe).

**Top up from NEAR** opens [Send to another chain](/docs/guide/send#to-another-chain) with USDC on Gnosis and the
card's address already filled in: NEAR Intents turns your USDC on NEAR into the USDC.e the card spends. Topping up
runs in Live; in the Sandbox the sheet says "Top-ups run in Live: NEAR Intents has no testnet."

## Customers who pay by card

Transak sells the customer USDC on NEAR, delivered straight to your account. On the [pay page](/docs/guide/pay-page)
the **Card** row reads "Visa, Mastercard · about 2 minutes". Open it and the customer sees, before anything starts:

| Line | |
| --- | --- |
| **Studio Kaze gets** | Exactly the amount due |
| **Card fee, set by Transak** | Transak's own quote, in euros |
| **You pay** | What the card is charged |

**Pay about €1,196.40 by card** opens Transak's checkout in the page, locked to USDC on NEAR, your account and the
exact amount. "Your card goes to Transak, not to Studio Kaze or Tegata." Then **Card charged**, **Sending as USDC on
NEAR** and **Checked on NEAR**, and your seal stamps PAID.

| | Sandbox | Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The card | Transak's test card, 4242 4242 4242 4242 · 10/33 · CVV 100 | A real card |
| Who delivers the USDC | Transak's staging checkout delivers nothing, so when the order completes, Tegata's operator sends the test USDC | Transak, on NEAR |
| Paid when | Tegata's own transfer lands, with the payment's memo | Tegata reads Transak's delivery transaction and finds a USDC transfer to you for at least the amount |
| The receipt says | **Card through:** Transak test checkout · test USDC sent by Tegata | **Card through:** Transak |

The same checkout adds money to your own balance: **Balance → Add money → With a card**, $100, $250 or $500, then
**Continue to Transak**.

<Callout title="Hidden until Transak's keys are set" type="info">
Customers never see the Card row until the server has Transak's keys. Your own **Add money → With a card** stays in
the list, greyed, with "Opening soon" (or, in Live, "Opens after Transak's business review").
</Callout>
