Open **Balance** under **Money**. The stage shows what you hold, read live from the USDC contract on NEAR (and what
sits in [autopilot](/docs/how-money-moves/autopilot), if any), your pay address, and three actions.

Turn on **Split money in** and choose a share for **Tax %** and **Savings %**, or a preset: **Freelancer** 25 · 10,
**Light** 15 · 5, **Savings only** 0 · 20. Together they can be at most 90%, so something is always left to spend. The
sheet works out an example as you type ("$1,000 in → $250 tax · $100 savings · $650 to spend"); **Split from now on**
saves it. Money already in stays as it is.

From then on, every payment that settles on NEAR sets its share aside, once per payment. Money marked paid by hand
never does: it isn't on the account. Pay that lands in a person's own Tegata account follows that person's own rule,
never their employer's.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| What a pot is | A label on your one NEAR account. The money never moves, so it needs no signature, Tegata holds nothing, and nothing is paid to a tax office |
| **To spend** | What you hold, less Tax and Savings |
| **To spending** | Moves money from a pot back to spending, up to what the pot holds |
| **Add** | Moves money from spending into a pot, up to what's spendable |
| If money left another way | When the account holds less than the pots (say, a transfer from a wallet outside Tegata), the pots shrink in proportion |

Once anything is set aside, Home shows **Spendable**, **Tax** and **Savings** under the balance, opening Balance.
Each mode, Sandbox and Live, has its own pots.

## Add money

<Phone src="/shots/phone/add-money.webp" alt="The Add money sheet on the live app in the Sandbox: To your pay address, With a card (Opening soon), From another chain (Live only), Test dollars ($5.00 of test USDC, once a day)" caption="Add money, in the Sandbox." eager />

**Add money** (also on Home) opens one sheet:

| Way | What it does | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To your pay address** | Shows your pay address and its QR code, for anyone sending USDC on NEAR | Both |
| **With a card** | Buy USDC on NEAR with a Visa or Mastercard through Transak: $100, $250 or $500, then **Continue to Transak**, fee shown first. See [Cards](/docs/guide/cards#customers-who-pay-by-card) | Once Transak's keys are set; until then "Opening soon" |
| **From another chain** | The dollars to add, then a coin and chain: a one-time pay page to your own account. See [Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain#add-money-from-another-chain) | Live only |
| **Test dollars** | $5.00 of test USDC, once a day | Sandbox only |

## Books against NEAR

Every USDC transfer on your account, read from NearBlocks, is matched by its transaction to what Tegata recorded.
A transfer that came or went another way (a faucet, a wallet outside Tegata) is listed as **Arrived outside
Tegata** or **Left outside Tegata**, and never matched automatically. **Check again** re-reads NEAR. More in
[If you keep the books](/docs/start/bookkeepers).
