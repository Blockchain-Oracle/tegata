**Get paid. Pay your team. In exact dollars.** Tegata gives a small business invoices, pay links and payroll that
settle in USDC on NEAR. The owner signs in with an email; Tegata opens a NEAR account named after the business and pays
every network fee, so nobody buys NEAR or keeps a wallet. Every payment gets a sealed, numbered receipt that links
to its transaction on NEAR.

## The problem

A small design studio finishes a job for a client abroad and sends an invoice for $1,250.00.

- **By card**, Stripe's published price (checked 12 Nov 2026) is 2.9% + 30¢, plus 1.5% for an international card and 1% when the
  currency is converted: about $68 of the $1,250.
- **By wire**, receiving US dollars into a Wise Business account costs $6.11, before whatever the client's bank
  charges to send it.
- **In stablecoins**, the studio has to ask which coin and chain the client holds, send an address for exactly
  that chain, match each incoming transfer to an invoice by its amount, and buy that chain's own token before
  the money can move again.

## The idea

The studio signs in with its email, and Tegata opens `studio.tegata.testnet` on NEAR for it. It
sends the invoice. The client opens the link and pays in USDC on NEAR, from a Tegata balance or any
NEAR wallet. The studio receives exactly $1,250.00. The invoice turns **Paid** when NEAR itself shows the
transfer, the studio's seal is stamped on it, and both sides can open the receipt.
