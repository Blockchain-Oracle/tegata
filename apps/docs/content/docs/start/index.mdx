**Get paid. Pay your team. In exact dollars.** Tegata is the money account for a small business, settled in USDC on
NEAR. The owner signs in with an email; Tegata opens a NEAR account named after the business and pays every network
fee, so nobody buys NEAR or keeps a wallet. Customers pay from a Tegata balance, any NEAR wallet or, in Live, a coin
on another chain (a card is next), and the business always receives exactly what it billed. Every payment gets a sealed, numbered
receipt that links to its transaction on NEAR.

## The problem

A small design studio finishes a job for a client abroad and sends an invoice for $1,250.00.

- **By card**, Stripe's published price (checked 12 Nov 2026) is 2.9% + 30¢, plus 1.5% for an international card and 1% when the
  currency is converted: about $68 of the $1,250.
- **By wire**, receiving US dollars into a Wise Business account costs $6.11, before whatever the client's bank
  charges to send it.
- **In stablecoins**, the studio has to ask which coin and chain the client holds, send an address for exactly
  that chain, match each incoming transfer to an invoice by its amount, and buy that chain's own token before
  the money can move again.

Paying the studio's own people is the same problem backwards: each one wants their pay somewhere different, and many
want it in their local bank.

## The idea

The studio signs in with its email, and Tegata opens `studio.tegata.near` on NEAR (`studio.tegata.testnet` in the
Sandbox). It sends the invoice. The client opens the link and pays the way they can: from a Tegata balance, any NEAR
wallet, or in Live USDT on Tron or another of 20 coin-and-chain routes on 11 chains (NEAR Intents converts it). The studio
receives exactly $1,250.00. The invoice turns **Paid** when NEAR itself shows the transfer, the studio's seal is
stamped on it, and both sides can open the receipt. One approval then pays the whole team, each person where they
chose.

## What works today

| Part | What it does | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoices | Customers, items, discount, tax, part-payments, repeat invoices, reminders, a PDF and a timeline | [Invoices](/docs/guide/invoices) |
| Pay links and the counter | A link for a fixed or any amount, with a QR code; a keypad for taking payments in person | [Pay links](/docs/guide/pay-links), [Counter](/docs/guide/counter) |
| The pay page | Pay from a Tegata balance or a NEAR wallet; in Live, from USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH or SOL on 11 chains | [The pay page](/docs/guide/pay-page) |
| Payroll | Your team by email or CSV; each person chooses where pay lands (in Live, another chain too); one approval pays everyone, or k of n approvers through a team treasury on NEAR; autopilot and team approvals in the Sandbox | [Pay people](/docs/guide/pay-people), [Team approvals](/docs/guide/team-approvals) |

| Cards | A Gnosis Pay card topped up from NEAR in Live. Built and waiting on partner keys: virtual Visa cards for the team (Bitnob) and customers paying by card (Transak) | [Cards](/docs/guide/cards) |
| Scan to pay | Any Tegata QR code, NEAR account or address on another chain | [Scan to pay](/docs/guide/scan) |
| Receipts and emails | A receipt for every payment, send, payslip and reward, and an email each time money moves | [Receipts](/docs/guide/receipts), [Emails](/docs/guide/emails) |
| Books | Activity by month, a CSV, a statement, refunds, and the books checked against NEAR | [If you keep the books](/docs/start/bookkeepers) |
| Developers | API keys, checkout sessions and signed webhooks | [For developers](/docs/build/developers) |
| Sandbox and Live | The whole app on NEAR testnet with test dollars, free; Live with real dollars on mainnet | [Sandbox and Live](/docs/guide/sandbox) |
