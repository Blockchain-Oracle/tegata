- **Dollars, not coins.** Every amount is in USDC, a digital dollar issued by Circle. One USDC is one US dollar.
- **Exact amounts.** An invoice for 1,250.00 is settled by exactly 1,250.00 reaching the business. Tegata takes no
  cut.
- **Numbers in order.** Invoices are numbered INV-0001, INV-0002… when they are sent; a voided invoice keeps its
  number. Receipts for customer payments are numbered No. 0001, No. 0002… per business.
- **A receipt for every movement**: customer payments, sends, payments to other chains, payslips and referral
  rewards.

## What a receipt says

| Line | For example |
| --- | --- |
| Paid to | Studio Kaze, `studio-kaze-4a050c1f.testnet` |
| For | Invoice INV-0001 |
| From | `northwind-aeb5ac54.tegata.testnet` |
| Paid with | NEAR wallet, Tegata balance, a coin on another chain ("USDT on Tron → USDC on NEAR"), Card, or Marked paid by hand (not on NEAR) |
| Settled | 13 Nov 2026, 17:11 GMT+1, in the reader's own time zone |
| Network fee | Paid by Tegata, or Paid by the payer |
| Transaction | <Tx hash="HxK167QKx4j4GSeE8Uzced781pmUgGZSXzmQTBCHShsF" />, opening on the NEAR explorer |

Money received another way (cash, a bank transfer) can be **marked paid by hand** on the invoice. Its receipt
says so, and it has no transaction.

## The month, for your books

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-activity.webp" alt="Activity for November 2026: money in $0.50, money out $0.25, net +$0.25, CSV and Statement buttons, filters, and the rows: Order #1042, a one-off payment, a payroll run, invoice INV-0001 and the Design sprint link" caption="Activity: the month's money in and out, every movement, and the two exports." address="tegata.xyz/app/activity" />

**Activity** shows one month at a time in the business's time zone, with money in, money out and net. Two exports:

| Export | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **CSV** | One row per movement: Date, Time, Type, Description, Counterparty, Counterparty account, Money in, Money out, Currency (USD), Status, Receipt, NEAR transaction. Plain decimals; a cell that starts with = + - @ is prefixed with ' so a spreadsheet never runs it |
| **Statement (PDF)** | The business header, money in, money out and net, every movement in order, and the balance on NEAR when it was made |

An autopilot payroll is listed but not added to the month's money out: its dollars left when they were set aside,
which is its own line. Each payment carries a mark for how it came in (a Tegata balance, a NEAR wallet, the coin it
was sent in, a card, or by hand), payments to other chains read like "USDT on Tron", and bank rows carry the
country's flag and the local money ("To Access Bank ••0031 · ₦…", "Bank transfer · €…").

**Balance** checks the books against NEAR: every USDC transfer on the account, read from NearBlocks, matched by its
transaction to what Tegata recorded. A transfer that came or went another way (a faucet, a wallet outside Tegata) is
listed as "outside Tegata" and never matched automatically.

A received payment can be **refunded**, all or part, back to the account that paid, in one gas-free transfer with its
own receipt; a payment from another chain is refunded in the payer's own coin to their refund address, in Live
([Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain#you-refund-a-payment-from-another-chain)). A real one: <Tx hash="BvQCXLWPsDegHGEtrpv2khqboY4rgtvb7jjuSVcr8e2n" /> paid $0.20, <Tx hash="BmQ3kF5UjvA1EkCvSwNYoUwW6gYfdbLfuDo1w3YRzu1Y" /> refunded $0.05.
