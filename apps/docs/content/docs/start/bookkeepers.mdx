- **Dollars, not coins.** Every amount is in USDC, a digital dollar issued by Circle. One USDC is one US dollar.
- **Exact amounts.** An invoice for 1,250.00 is settled by exactly 1,250.00 reaching the business. Tegata takes no
  cut.
- **Numbers in order.** Invoices are numbered INV-0001, INV-0002… when they are sent; a voided invoice keeps its
  number. Receipts for customer payments are numbered No. 0001, No. 0002… per business.
- **A receipt for every movement**: customer payments, sends and referral rewards.

## What a receipt says

| Line | For example |
| --- | --- |
| Paid to | Abu Studio, `abu-studio.tegata.testnet` |
| For | Invoice INV-0001 |
| From | `tegata.testnet` |
| Paid with | NEAR wallet, Tegata balance, or Marked paid by hand (not on NEAR) |
| Settled | 12 Nov 2026, 17:40 GMT+1, in the reader's own time zone |
| Network fee | Paid by Tegata, or Paid by the payer's wallet |
| Transaction | `4DaV5L…FQCquE`, opening on the NEAR explorer |

Money received another way (cash, a bank transfer) can be **marked paid by hand** on the invoice. Its receipt
says so, and it has no transaction.

## The month, for your books

<Shot src="/shots/wide/app-activity.webp" alt="Activity for November 2026: money in $0.50, money out $0.25, net +$0.25, CSV and Statement buttons, filters, and the rows: Order #1042, a one-off payment, a payroll run, invoice INV-0001 and the Design sprint link" caption="Activity: the month's money in and out, every movement, and the two exports." address="tegata.84.46.247.92.sslip.io/app/activity" />

**Activity** shows one month at a time in the business's time zone, with money in, money out and net. Two exports:

| Export | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **CSV** | One row per movement: Date, Time, Type, Description, Counterparty, Counterparty account, Money in, Money out, Currency (USD), Status, Receipt, NEAR transaction. Plain decimals; a cell that starts with = + - @ is prefixed with ' so a spreadsheet never runs it |
| **Statement (PDF)** | The business header, money in, money out and net, every movement in order, and the balance on NEAR when it was made |

An autopilot payroll is listed but not added to the month's money out: its dollars left when they were set aside,
which is its own line.

**Balance** checks the books against NEAR: every USDC transfer on the account, read from NearBlocks, matched by its
transaction to what Tegata recorded. A transfer that came or went another way (a faucet, a wallet outside Tegata) is
listed as "outside Tegata" and never matched automatically.

A received payment can be **refunded**, all or part, back to the account that paid, in one gas-free transfer with its
own receipt. A real one: <Tx hash="BvQCXLWPsDegHGEtrpv2khqboY4rgtvb7jjuSVcr8e2n" /> paid $0.20, <Tx hash="BmQ3kF5UjvA1EkCvSwNYoUwW6gYfdbLfuDo1w3YRzu1Y" /> refunded $0.05.
