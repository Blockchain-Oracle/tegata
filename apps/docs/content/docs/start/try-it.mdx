---
title: Try it in five minutes
description: Follow eight steps on the live Sandbox and reach a paid invoice with a sealed receipt, with no wallet and no money.
icon: Play
---

The Sandbox is the whole app on NEAR testnet with test USDC, and it's free. You need two email addresses, one for
the business and one for its customer (`you+customer@gmail.com` reaches the same Gmail inbox), and two browser
windows: a normal one and a private one.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open your business account

Open [the app](https://tegata.xyz/signin), enter your email, then the six-digit code it sends.
The code cells turn vermilion and Tegata's seal presses.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name your business and pick a pay address

Press **Open my account**. `your-name.tegata.testnet` is created on NEAR and registered for USDC while you watch,
then your business's own seal is stamped.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send an invoice

Open **Invoices**, press **New invoice**. Add a customer and one item for $1.00, press **Review and send**, then
**Send invoice**. The invoice gets its number (INV-0001) and a link. Press **Copy link**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open it as your customer

Open the link in the private window: the invoice as your customer sees it, with items, total, a PDF and
**Pay $1.00**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign in as the customer

Press **Pay $1.00**, choose **Tegata balance**, and sign in with the second email; name its business and open its
account. The pay page comes back, signed in as the customer.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add test dollars

Press **Add $5.00 test dollars**. 5 test USDC arrive from Tegata's Sandbox stash.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pay

Press and hold **Hold to pay $1.00**. Live steps, then your business's seal stamps PAID. **View your receipt**
opens receipt No. 0001 with its NEAR transaction.

</Step>
<Step>

### See it paid

Back in the first window, open the invoice: **Paid**, the payment on its timeline, and on Home the new balance with
the payment under Latest. Every row there opens its receipt.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="If the stash is empty" type="warn">
If **Add test dollars** says "The Sandbox stash is empty right now", get 20 test USDC from
[Circle's faucet](https://faucet.circle.com): choose NEAR Testnet, paste the customer's pay address, then hold to
pay.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Then try Live" type="info">
Tap **Live** at the top of the app to open your `name.tegata.near` account on NEAR mainnet, where customers can pay
from USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH or SOL on 11 chains. It's real money: see [Sandbox and Live](/docs/guide/sandbox).
</Callout>
