Every balance in Tegata is USDC on NEAR. NEAR Intents' 1Click API is the bridge to everything else: it swaps a coin
on another chain into USDC on NEAR, and back. Tegata uses it four ways, all in [Live](/docs/guide/sandbox):

| | Direction | Quote | If it can't complete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A customer pays you | In | **Exact output**: you receive exactly the amount due | The payer is refunded on their own chain |
| You send to another chain | Out | **Exact input**: you send exactly what you approved; they receive about the quoted amount | The USDC comes back to your balance |
| You refund a payment from another chain | Out | Exact input, to the payer's refund address | Back to your balance |
| A payroll row on another chain | Out | Quoted when you approve the run: **exact output** for USDT and USDC (they get exactly their pay), exact input for BTC, ETH and SOL | Back to your balance; the row can be paid again |

NEAR Intents has no testnet. In the Sandbox the pay page still shows NEAR Intents' real, live price and stops there;
sending, refunds, payroll and adding money from another chain are locked. Each is marked "Live only".

## The routes

| Coin | Chains |
| --- | --- |
| USDT | Tron, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, Polygon, Avalanche, Gnosis, and USDT0 (Tether's omnichain USDT) on Arbitrum |
| USDC | Base, Solana, Arbitrum, Polygon, Gnosis, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Sui |
| BTC | Bitcoin |
| ETH | Ethereum |
| SOL | Solana |

Twenty routes on eleven chains (Tron, Base, Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, Bitcoin, BNB Chain, Polygon, Gnosis, Avalanche,
Sui), fixed in code (`apps/web/lib/routes.ts`); each one's asset is looked up in 1Click's token list. The pay page,
Send and a person's **Another chain** share one picker: the coin first (USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, each with how many
chains it's on), then the chain. USDT0 sits with USDT, and counts as a dollar coin. USDC on Gnosis is the coin a Gnosis Pay card spends, so [topping up a card](/docs/guide/cards#a-crypto-card-you-already-have)
is a send to Gnosis. 1Click sets its own minimums per route, and Tegata says them in dollars: "USDC on Base needs at
least $0.15 right now", "USDT on Tron needs at least $100 right now".

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose a coin and give a refund address

On the [pay page](/docs/guide/pay-page), **Another chain** shows the coins, and for the chosen one "You would send"
with a **Live quote**. The payer types a **Refund address on Tron** (or whichever chain) and, if they want, **Email me
a receipt (optional)**, then presses **Get the deposit address**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Tegata asks for a signed quote, and checks it

The quote is `EXACT_OUTPUT`: the business receives exactly the amount due as USDC on NEAR, and the route's cost sits
on the payer's side. Slippage is 0.1% for dollar coins and 1% for the rest; refunds go to the payer's address on their
own chain; the deadline is 20 minutes. Tegata shows the payer a quote only if 1Click signed it and its recipient,
amount, destination (USDC on NEAR), type and deposit address all match what was asked.
</Step>
<Step>
### The payer sends

The panel says **Send exactly** the amount, **Pay within** a countdown, the coin and chain, **To this address**, a
memo where the chain needs one, and a QR code. On Ethereum-style chains, Bitcoin and SOL the QR code carries the
amount ("Amount filled in"); on Tron and for USDC on Solana it carries the address only ("Address only · type the
amount").
</Step>
<Step>
### NEAR Intents converts, NEAR settles

The steps read **Waiting for your USDT**, **Seen on Tron**, **Converting on NEAR Intents** and **Paid to Studio Kaze on
NEAR**. Tegata's worker reads 1Click every 5 seconds. The payment counts only when 1Click reports success **and** the
delivery transaction on NEAR holds a USDC transfer to the business for at least the amount due.
</Step>
</Steps>

| What happens | What the payer and you see |
| --- | --- |
| Too little arrives | "Less than 1,251.387 USDT arrived. Send the rest to the same address before 15:58, or it's refunded." |
| It can't complete in time | NEAR Intents refunds the payer on their own chain. The payment shows the refund, and you get an email: "A $… payment from … didn't complete. The payer was refunded" |
| Nothing arrives | 72 hours after the window, the payment closes as expired |

The receipt reads **Paid with:** USDT on Tron → USDC on NEAR, **Converted by:** NEAR Intents, **Network fee:** Paid
by the payer, and **From** is the payer's refund address. In Activity the row carries the coin the payer actually
sent.

**Send → Another chain**: pick the coin, then the chain, paste **Their address on Base** (or another chain), and the
**Live quote from NEAR Intents** shows what they get. The review says **You send exactly**, **They receive** about,
**Arrives**, **Converted by** NEAR Intents and **Network fee: Paid by Tegata**.

Press and hold **Hold to send**. Your key signs one gas-free USDC transfer to the quote's deposit address, a NEAR
account 1Click made for this send. Before signing, the page checks that transfer against the quote; Tegata's operator
registers the deposit address for USDC (0.00125 NEAR) and pays the fee. The steps read **Approved with your key**,
**Sent from your balance**, **Picked up by NEAR Intents** and **Delivered on Tron**, and the receipt links both
transactions. If NEAR Intents can't deliver within 30 minutes, the USDC comes back: "$240.00 is back in your
balance."

## You refund a payment from another chain

In **Activity**, a payment that came from another chain refunds the way it came. The **Refund** sheet shows how much
can go back, and "USDT on Tron to" the payer's refund address; **Hold to refund** sends it out as above. It counts
against the payment once it's delivered. A refund to a NEAR account is refused for these payments: "Paid from
another chain: refund it to the payer's chain".

## Your team, paid on another chain

In Live, a person can choose **Another chain** on their pay page ("USDT, USDC, BTC… through NEAR Intents"): a coin
and chain and their address there. You can set it for them with **Pay them on another chain**. See
[Pay people](/docs/guide/pay-people#where-their-pay-lands).

When you approve a run, each chain row gets its own fresh, signed quote, and its USDC goes to that quote's deposit
address inside the same signed batch as everyone else's.

- **USDT or USDC** on another chain: the quote is exact output, so the person receives exactly their pay, and you
  cover NEAR Intents' conversion on top: "Rows on another chain arrive in full. NEAR Intents' conversion, at most 2%
  or $1 each, is added to what you send." The limit is 2% of the pay or $1, whichever is more, checked again in your
  browser before you sign; a route that costs more is named instead of paid.
- **BTC, ETH or SOL**: exact input. The pay leaves your balance, and they receive its worth at the quoted rate.

After the hold, a chain row reads **On its way** until NEAR Intents reports it delivered; only then is it **Paid**,
with its payslip (adding **Delivered as**) and its email. If NEAR Intents sends it back, the row reads **Not paid**,
the USDC is back in your balance, the run says "…'s pay couldn't be delivered on Tron; the USDC came back to your
balance. Pay again to send it.", and paying the run again sends only that row.

[Autopilot](/docs/how-money-moves/autopilot) never pays chain rows: a quote needs someone to approve it.

## Add money from another chain

**Balance → Add money → From another chain** asks for the dollars to add, then opens a one-time pay page to your own
account with **Another chain** ready: the same flow as a customer's, paid by you.

| Piece | Code |
| --- | --- |
| Routes | `apps/web/lib/routes.ts` |
| Live prices (dry quotes) and QR payment links | `apps/web/lib/server/oneclick.ts` |
| Pay-ins: quote, check, follow, settle | `packages/services/src/oneclick-payments.ts` |
| Sends, refunds and their delivery | `packages/services/src/payouts.ts` |
| Payroll rows on another chain | `packages/services/src/payroll.ts` (`prepareRun`, `settleRunRelay`) |
| The 1Click client and quote signature check | `packages/core/src/oneclick` |
