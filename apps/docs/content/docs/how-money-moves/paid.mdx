## The check

When the payer starts paying, the pay page opens an attempt with its own memo, `tegata:pay:<id>`. The transfer
carries that memo whichever way it's paid. Then the worker:

1. waits until the transaction is **final** on NEAR;
2. reads the USDC contract's own `ft_transfer` event from it;
3. checks the **receiver** is the business, the **amount** covers what's due, and the **memo** is this attempt's;
4. only then marks the payment paid, updates the link or invoice, and numbers the receipt.

Nothing the payer's browser says is believed: a NEAR wallet's transaction hash is only a hint of where to look.

## What follows

- **Part-payments.** An invoice that allows them takes any amount up to what's left; a payment above that is refused
  before anything is sent. INV-0001 ($0.50) was paid $0.20, then refused at $0.40 ("The most left to pay is $0.30"),
  then paid $0.30.
- **Numbers.** Receipt numbers come from a per-business counter taken in the same database transaction as the
  payment; invoice numbers come from one taken when the invoice is sent. Neither can repeat.
- **By hand.** Money received outside NEAR can be marked paid by hand on the invoice. Its receipt says it isn't on
  NEAR, and it never counts toward a referral.
- **The seal.** The business's seal stamps the invoice and the receipt, and the `received` sound plays once per
  payment.

| --- | --- |
