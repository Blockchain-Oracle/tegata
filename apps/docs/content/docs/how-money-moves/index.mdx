Four rules hold everything up:

1. **Money is USDC on NEAR.** Circle's native USDC (NEP-141) holds every balance and settles every payment, send,
   payslip and reward, on NEAR testnet in the Sandbox and NEAR mainnet in Live.
2. **The owner signs; Tegata relays and pays.** Each action is a NEP-366 delegate action signed by the owner's own
   key; Tegata's worker sends it and pays the network fee. See [Accounts without gas](/docs/how-money-moves/gas-free).
3. **NEAR decides "paid".** A payment counts only when the USDC contract's own transfer event, read from the final
   transaction, matches it. Money received another way can be marked paid by hand, and its receipt says so. See
   [When is it paid?](/docs/how-money-moves/paid)
4. **Everything else meets NEAR at the edge.** Other chains through NEAR Intents, banks through Noah, spend cards
   through Bitnob and card payments through Transak, each converting to or from USDC on NEAR. See
   [Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain) and [Bank and cards](/docs/how-money-moves/bank-and-cards).

## Every movement carries a memo

| Movement | Memo on the USDC transfer | Receipt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A customer pays a link or an invoice | `tegata:pay:<payment id>` | Numbered, `/r/<token>` |
| A customer pays from another chain (Live) | None: NEAR Intents delivers it; Tegata matches its delivery transaction | Numbered, `/r/<token>` |
| Tegata pays a referral reward | `tegata:referral:<referral id>` | `/r/reward/<id>` |
| Tegata sends Sandbox test dollars | `tegata:test-dollars` | none (test money) |
| You send dollars | `tegata:send` (or `tegata:<your note>`) | `/r/send/<id>` |
| A payroll run pays a person | `tegata:payroll:<row>` | `/r/slip/<row>` |
| You refund a payment on NEAR | `tegata:refund:<payment id>` | `/r/send/<id>` |
| You withdraw to a bank | `tegata:bank:<id>` (to Tegata's operator); `tegata:bankback:<id>` if it comes back | The slip on Bank |
| A bank deposit is credited | `tegata:bankin:<id>` (from Tegata's operator) | The row on Bank |
| You fill a spend card | `tegata:card:<move>` (to Tegata's card reserve); `tegata:cardback:<id>` if Bitnob refuses | The card's page |
| Money marked paid by hand | nothing on NEAR | Numbered, says "not on NEAR" |

## A payment's life

From a NEAR wallet: `awaiting` → `detected` → `succeeded` or `failed`. From a Tegata balance: `awaiting` →
`succeeded` or `failed`. From another chain: `awaiting` → `detected` (seen, then converting) → `succeeded`, or
`refunded`, `failed` or `expired`. By card: `awaiting` → `settling` (charged, the USDC on its way) → `succeeded`
or `failed`. Only succeeded payments count toward a link or an invoice; a request is paid when
they add up to its total.
