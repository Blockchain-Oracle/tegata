A business is always paid an exact amount. So every price for paying in is an **exact-output** quote: the business
receives exactly $1,250.00 in USDC on NEAR, and the payer's side carries the cost of the route.

| Coin | Chains |
| --- | --- |
| USDT | Tron, Ethereum, BNB Chain |
| USDC | Base, Solana, Arbitrum, Polygon, Gnosis |
| BTC | Bitcoin |
| ETH | Ethereum |
| SOL | Solana |

- The eleven routes are fixed in code (`apps/web/lib/routes.ts`); each one's asset is looked up in 1Click's
  `/v0/tokens` (cached for 5 minutes).
- A price shown before anyone commits is a **dry** quote, cached for 30 seconds. A dry quote never creates a deposit
  address. The landing page's calculator and the pay page's **Another chain** show one everywhere; Send's
  **Another chain** shows one in Live.
- When 1Click won't route an amount, the box shows its reason. On 13 Nov 2026 its live answer for small amounts was
  "Temporary swap limits: minimum swap amount is $100".
- In Live, **Get the deposit address** asks for a real, signed quote with a deposit address and a 20-minute window,
  and Tegata checks it before the payer sees it: [Any chain, in and out](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain).

NEAR Intents has no testnet. In the Sandbox the price is real and marked "Live only · real price"; the deposit
address, the conversion and the delivery happen in Live.
</Callout>

Code: `apps/web/lib/server/oneclick.ts` and `GET /api/quote` for prices; `packages/services/src/oneclick-payments.ts`
for paying in.
