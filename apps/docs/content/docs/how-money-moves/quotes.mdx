<Shot src="/shots/wide/landing.webp" alt="Tegata's landing page: Get paid. Pay your team. In exact dollars, beside the calculator: a payer on Tron sends 1,263.93 USDT for you to receive exactly $1,250.00" caption="The landing page's calculator, on live quotes." address="tegata.xyz" />

A business is always paid an exact amount. So every price is an **exact-output** quote: the business receives
exactly $1,250.00 in USDC on NEAR, and the payer's side carries the cost of the route.

| Route | Coin |
| --- | --- |
| Tron | USDT |
| Base | USDC |
| Ethereum | USDT, ETH |
| Solana | USDC, SOL |
| Arbitrum | USDC |
| Bitcoin | BTC |
| BNB Chain | USDT |
| Polygon | USDC |

- The ten routes are fixed in code; each one's asset is looked up in 1Click's `/v0/tokens` (cached for 5
  minutes).
- Each price is a **dry** `EXACT_OUTPUT` quote, cached for 30 seconds. A dry quote never creates a deposit address.
- You see them on the landing page's calculator and on the pay page under **Another chain**. When 1Click won't
  route an amount, the box shows its reason, such as a $100 minimum.

<Phone src="/shots/phone/pay-other-chain.webp" alt="The pay page with Another chain open on a $0.25 link: the coin grid, and the live quote box showing 1Click's $100 minimum" caption="On a $0.25 link, 1Click's live answer is its $100 minimum." />

<Callout title="Where this stops today" type="warn">
NEAR Intents has no testnet. In the Sandbox, paying from another chain stops at the price; the deposit address that
turns a payer's coin into USDC on NEAR is part of Live, and isn't built yet.
</Callout>

Code: `apps/web/lib/server/oneclick.ts` and `GET /api/quote`.
