A business's money stays USDC on NEAR, in the business's own account. Banks and card networks need a licensed
partner, so Tegata uses one for each, at the edge, and keeps every step that can be on NEAR on NEAR.

| Partner | What it does here | Today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Noah** | Pays out to banks in Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa; opens euro accounts whose deposits arrive as USDC | Sandbox. Rates, fees, bank lists, account names, euro accounts and deposits are Noah's real Sandbox answers. Payouts to a bank aren't switched on for Tegata's sandbox account yet |
| **Bitnob** | Issues virtual Visa cards and reports every purchase | Built; waits on Bitnob's sandbox keys. **Cards** says "Opening soon" |
| **Transak** | Sells a card holder USDC on NEAR, delivered to the business | Built; waits on Transak's keys. The Card way to pay stays hidden until then |
| **NEAR Intents** | Swaps between USDC on NEAR and coins on other chains | Live. See [Any chain](/docs/how-money-moves/any-chain) |

## A withdrawal to a bank

| Step | What happens | Who holds the money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review | Noah prepares the sale and names the account holder; Tegata sets Noah's payout rule for the exact amount, from Tegata's Solana wallet (it expires after 6 days) | The business |
| Hold to withdraw | One gas-free USDC transfer from the business to Tegata's operator, memo `tegata:bank:<id>` | Tegata's operator |
| The hop | Once the transfer is read on NEAR, Tegata's own Solana devnet wallet sends the same amount of Noah's test USDC to Noah's deposit address | Noah |
| The bank | Noah sells it and pays the bank in local money; Tegata's worker checks Noah every 8 seconds until it's paid or has failed | The recipient's bank |
| If it fails | Tegata's operator sends the USDC back, memo `tegata:bankback:<id>` | The business |

Why Solana: Noah takes USDC on Solana, and in Live the hop from NEAR to Solana is NEAR Intents. NEAR Intents has no
testnet, so in the Sandbox Tegata's own wallet makes that hop with Noah's test USDC (a Token-2022 mint on Solana
devnet). The NEAR leg and the Noah leg are both real.

## A deposit into a euro account

| Step | What happens | Who holds the money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank details | Noah opens a euro virtual account for the business that delivers to Tegata's Solana wallet | |
| A transfer arrives | Noah buys USDC with it and sends it to Tegata's Solana wallet | Tegata's Solana wallet |
| Credit | Every minute the worker credits each settled Noah deposit once: Tegata's operator sends the same dollars in USDC on NEAR to the business, memo `tegata:bankin:<id>` | The business |

In the Sandbox those dollars come from Tegata's own test-USDC stash; if it's short, the deposit is marked failed: "Tegata's
Sandbox stash can't cover this deposit". A real one: <Tx hash="CGQr6SEf3P5spDqohfH91PGCMABGjA1RGDpiYyczHe6F" />,
€2.00 credited as 1.16 USDC.

## A spend card

| Step | What happens | Who holds the money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fill | One gas-free USDC transfer from the business to Tegata's card reserve (the operator account), memo `tegata:card:<move>`, at least $2.00 | The card reserve |
| Card | Once the transfer is read on NEAR, the worker asks Bitnob to issue the card with that first fill, or to raise it. If Bitnob refuses, the USDC goes back (`tegata:cardback:`) | The USDC stays in the reserve; Bitnob holds the card's balance |
| Purchases | Bitnob calls `POST /api/webhooks/bitnob`; each call must carry an HMAC-SHA512 signature of its body under Tegata's webhook secret, and each event is kept once | |
| Move back | Bitnob lowers the card, then the operator sends the USDC back to the business | The business |

The card number never touches Tegata.

## A card payment

| | Sandbox | Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checkout | Transak's staging checkout, test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 | Transak's checkout, a real card |
| Who sends the USDC | Transak's staging orders deliver nothing on NEAR, so once the order is complete, Tegata's operator sends the test USDC with the payment's memo | Transak, from its own wallet, straight to the business |
| How Tegata decides "paid" | The same check as any payment on NEAR: the transfer, the amount and the memo | Tegata reads the transaction Transak reports and finds a USDC transfer to the business for at least the amount |
| Custody | Tegata's operator (test money) | None: Transak to the business |

Transak sends no webhook here: Tegata follows each order every 5 seconds (and sweeps open ones every minute), and an
order whose reference names another payment is refused.

## Where Tegata holds money

| Flow | The money's path |
| --- | --- |
| A payment from a Tegata balance or a NEAR wallet | Payer → business |
| A payment from another chain | Payer → NEAR Intents → business |
| A card payment, Live | Transak → business |
| A send, a refund, payroll | Business → recipient (through NEAR Intents for another chain) |
| Autopilot | Business → Tegata's contract, `pay.tegata.testnet` → people |
| A bank withdrawal | Business → **Tegata's operator** → Tegata's Solana wallet → Noah → bank |
| A euro deposit | Noah → **Tegata's Solana wallet**; Tegata's operator credits the business |
| A spend card | Business → **Tegata's card reserve** → Bitnob card |

Money on its way to a bank or a card passes through an account Tegata controls for the minutes it takes; everywhere
else it moves from the business's own account.

| Needs | Variables (server) |
| --- | --- |
| Noah | `NOAH_API_KEY`; the Solana hop: `SOLANA_DEVNET_ADDRESS`, and `SOLANA_DEVNET_SECRET` on the worker |
| Bitnob | `BITNOB_CLIENT_ID`, `BITNOB_CLIENT_SECRET`, `BITNOB_WEBHOOK_SECRET` |
| Transak | `TRANSAK_API_KEY`, `TRANSAK_API_SECRET`, `TRANSAK_ENV` (staging by default) |

Each is optional: without its keys, that part of the app says so and the rest works.

Code: `packages/services/src/bank.ts`, `issued-cards.ts`, `card-payments.ts`; the clients in `packages/core/src/noah`,
`bitnob`, `transak`, `solana` and `gnosis`.
